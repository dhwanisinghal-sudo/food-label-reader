## Sample-size caveat

This is a **15-photo** evaluation. Treat it as a directional smoke test, not a benchmark:
- Fields within one photo are correlated (a blurry photo fails on almost every field at once), so the effective sample is closer to 15 than to the 155 individual fields, and the overall figure could plausibly move by 10+ points on a different set of photos. Re-running `evaluate_accuracy.py` adds a bootstrap 95% interval (resampling photos) to this section.
- Per-field rows rest on even fewer photos (see the Expected column); one photo moves a row by several points. `added_sugars_g` is based on only 8 photos.
- The photos are a hand-picked mix of clean shots, angled shots and two-column labels, not a random sample of real-world labels.
- The before/after comparison in the README uses the same photos the fixes were developed against, so the improvement is likely overstated for unseen photos. A held-out set of photos not used during development is the next step.
